# Empty Hand Tai Chi (Taijiquan) vs Tai Chi Sword (Taijijian)

Tai Chi sword (*taijijian*) is the straight sword weapon branch inside traditional *taijiquan* rather than a separate martial art: it applies the exact same rooted weight shifts, relaxed *song* alignment, and waist driven silk reeling through an unsharpened double edged straight sword.

* **Symmetry vs Cantilever Extension:** Empty hand routines load both arms symmetrically in a `6 by 6 ft` space, whereas sword routines extend intent (*yi*) to the rounded tip of a `28, 30, or 32 inch` heat treated `2Cr13` stainless blade (`around 1.30, 1.32, or 1.39 lbs / approx. 590, 600, or 630 g` bare weight; balance about `2.4, 3.3, or 3.5 inches` past the guard) while the left hand forms counterbalancing sword fingers (*jian zhi*) across an `8 by 10 ft` clear floor area.
* **Instant Alignment Mirror:** Minor wrist stiffness that hides during empty hand practice immediately reveals itself as tip wobble or tassel tangling on the blade, accelerating whole body coordination.
