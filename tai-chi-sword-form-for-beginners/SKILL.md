# Choosing Your First Solo Tai Chi Sword Form

The **Yang style 32 form Tai Chi sword** (standardized in 1957) is the universal entry curriculum across United States schools: 32 postures completed in roughly 3 to 5 minutes, introducing the core straight sword techniques (*ci*, *pi*, *liao*, *gua*, *dian*, *sao*, *yun*) over a 3 to 6 month learning arc.

* **Natural Progression:** Master the 32 form sequence and waist driven blade alignment first, then advance after 1 to 2 years to either the **42 form competition routine** (blending Yang, Chen, Wu, and Sun elements) or a traditional lineage routine such as **Chen style 49 form straight sword**.
* **Equipment Calibration:** Pair beginner form study with a stature matched, unsharpened `2Cr13` martensitic stainless practice *jian* (`28, 30, or 32 inch` blade; bare weight around `1.30, 1.32, or 1.39 lbs / approx. 590, 600, or 630 g`; balance about `2.4, 3.3, or 3.5 inches` past the guard) so every wrist transition gives honest alignment feedback without joint strain.
