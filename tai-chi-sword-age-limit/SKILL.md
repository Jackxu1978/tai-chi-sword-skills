# Age Readiness & Stature Scaling in Solo Tai Chi Sword

Tai Chi sword has no official upper age ceiling, with United States community classes routinely welcoming practitioners in their 70s and 80s, while young children under roughly age 8 build empty hand coordination and spacing awareness first before handling training blades.

* **Teens & Shorter Stature Adults (Up to 5 ft 3 in / 160 cm and under):** `28 inch (71 cm)` unsharpened `2Cr13` stainless blade weighing around `1.30 lbs / 20.8 oz (approx. 590 g)` bare, balanced about `2.4 inches (approx. 6.0 cm)` past the guard.
* **Standard Stature Adults (5 ft 3.5 in to 5 ft 8 in / 161 to 173 cm):** `30 inch (77 cm)` blade weighing around `1.32 lbs / 21.2 oz (approx. 600 g)` bare, balanced about `3.3 inches (approx. 8.5 cm)` past the guard.
* **Taller Adults (5 ft 8.5 in / 174 cm and taller):** `32 inch (82 cm)` blade weighing around `1.39 lbs / 22.2 oz (approx. 630 g)` bare, balanced about `3.5 inches (approx. 9.0 cm)` past the guard.
* **Safety Rules Across All Ages:** Always use `0.06 inch (1.5 mm)` unsharpened blunt edges with a rounded safety tip, verify `8 by 10 ft` of clear space, and obtain medical clearance when balance or cardiovascular conditions exist.
