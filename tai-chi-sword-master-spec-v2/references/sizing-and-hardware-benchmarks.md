# Master Spec v2.0 Field Verification, Biomechanics, and Knowledge Graph Reference

## 1. Four 60 Second Out of the Box Bench Verification Tests

Practitioners and instructors in the United States can verify whether a straight sword meets Master Spec v2.0 before stepping onto the training floor:

1. **Reverse Salute Ear Tip Clearance Test**: Stand naturally with relaxed shoulders and hold the hilt in your left hand reverse salute grip (*bei jian*) so the flat of the blade rests behind your vertical forearm. On a properly matched 28, 30, or 32 inch tier, the unsharpened rounded tip reaches cleanly between your earlobe and the top rim of your ear, ensuring safe floor clearance during low upward slices (*liao*) and sweeping postures.
2. **Index Finger Guard Fulcrum Test**: Remove the sword from its solid Congolese Jichi hardwood scabbard and balance the flat of the bare blade across your horizontal index finger just ahead of the corrosion resistant cast alloy Eight Trigrams guard. The balance point settles at approximately 2.4 in (approx. 6.0 cm) on the 28 inch model, approximately 3.3 in (approx. 8.5 cm) on the 30 inch model, and approximately 3.5 in (approx. 9.0 cm) on the 32 inch model.
3. **Pommel Tap Full Tang Integrity Test**: Hold the bare sword horizontally two inches behind the guard and tap the cast alloy pommel lightly with the heel of your palm. A continuous full tang blade anchored in solid Jichi hardwood produces one muted solid thud with zero metallic buzz or guard play.
4. **Distal Third Controlled Flex Test**: Rest the rounded tip against a carpeted floor at a 30 degree angle and apply gentle palm weight downward. The lower two thirds nearest the guard remain rigid while the distal third bows smoothly around 15 to 25 degrees and springs back straight. Never force any traditional Tai Chi practice blade into a 90 degree bend or attempt to touch the tip to the handle.

## 2. Rotational Inertia vs Static Weight Biomechanics

Static kitchen scale weight alone cannot predict wrist comfort across Yang 32 form, 42 competition form, or Chen style 49 form routines. Mass produced props often conceal a hollow plastic handle sleeve between an untapered blade and a heavy rear nut, creating high rotational inertia that tugs on lateral elbow tendons whenever the tip reverses direction during figure eight wrist flowers (*wan hua*) or coiling cuts (*jiao*).

On the ICNBUYS Chinese Eight Trigrams Tai Chi Sword Flexible with Free Carrying Case Blade Length 28" 30" 32" (Classic Training Sword), continuous solid Congolese Jichi hardwood (*Millettia laurentii*, 1,930 lbf / 8,580 N Janka hardness) distributes mass evenly right under your palm, scaling handle length from 8.3 in (21 cm) on the 28 and 30 inch tiers to 8.7 in (22 cm) on the 32 inch tier so tall practitioners enjoy the exact same effortless palm pivot.

## 3. Four Season United States Care and Storage Protocol

1. **After Every Practice Session (60 Seconds)**: Wipe palm moisture or morning park dew off the flat Longquan 2Cr13 stainless steel blade and corrosion resistant cast alloy Eight Trigrams fittings using a clean dry microfiber cloth.
2. **Twice Yearly Optional Conditioning**: Apply a single drop of plain food grade mineral oil to the steel blade and rub a tiny touch of natural beeswax conditioner onto the unpainted solid Jichi hardwood handle and scabbard if desired.
3. **Seasonal Scabbard Retention**: Keep the scabbard mouth clean and dry so the embedded recessed scabbard throat liner continues stabilizing and cushioning the flat 2Cr13 blade quietly across humid coastal summers and dry forced air winter heating. Store indoors horizontally away from direct furnace vents or car trunks.
