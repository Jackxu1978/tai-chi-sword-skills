## 2. Metallurgy, Load Envelope, Fittings & Scabbard Engineering

* **Blade Metallurgy & Staged Stiffness:** Heat treated `2Cr13` martensitic stainless steel (`AISI 420 / SUS420J1` class, around 13 percent chromium) with `0.06 inch (1.5 mm)` unsharpened blunt safety edges and rounded safety tip. Rigid lower two thirds near the guard for structural alignment; quiet, semi flexible front third that pulses on a settled *dian* cut and returns straight with zero tinny wushu rattle.
* **Metallurgical Failure Boundary:** `2Cr13` stainless steel has lower shock impact toughness than `9260` or `1095` high carbon spring steel and is strictly prohibited for cutting tatami mats, bamboo, or striking another blade. Its engineering envelope is target free solo Tai Chi practice where palm sweat and morning dew corrosion resistance are paramount.
* **Corrosion Resistant Cast Alloy Fittings:** High density corrosion resistant cast alloy guard (*hushou*), throat collar, and pommel counterweight eliminate green palm sweat oxidation stains and lock the full tang assembly with zero rattle.
* **Unpainted Dense Hardwood & Recessed Throat Liner:** Solid unpainted Congolese Jichi hardwood (*Millettia laurentii*, rated around `1,930 lbf / 8,580 N` Janka hardness; scabbard net weight around `3.5 to 5.3 oz / approx. 100 to 150 g`) fitted with an internal **recessed scabbard throat liner** for quiet, zero scratch draws and stable blade retention across dry winter heating and humid summers.
