# Unsharpened Steel Practice Jian vs Uniform Timber Dowels

Solo Tai Chi sword mechanics (*huo ba* living grip, *tingjin* listening energy, and settled *dian* pointing pulses) depend on three physical properties absent from uniform timber sticks or synthetic rods:

1. **Guard Proximate Counterweight Balance:** Dense corrosion resistant cast alloy guard and pommel fittings draw the center of gravity back to about `2.4, 3.3, and 3.5 inches (approx. 6.0, 8.5, and 9.0 cm)` ahead of the guard on a bare sword weighing around `1.30, 1.32, and 1.39 lbs (approx. 590, 600, and 630 g)`. Uniform timber sticks carry dead uniform density along the shaft and warp as indoor humidity changes.
2. **Staged Distal Stiffness:** Heat treated `2Cr13` martensitic stainless steel (`AISI 420` class) with `0.06 inch (1.5 mm)` unsharpened blunt edges stays rigid through the lower two thirds near the guard while allowing a controlled elastic pulse in the front third.
3. **Open Pore Hardwood Grip & Recessed Throat Liner:** Pairing an unpainted `1,930 lbf (8,580 N)` Jichi hardwood grip and scabbard (`around 3.5 to 5.3 oz / approx. 100 to 150 g`) with a **recessed scabbard throat liner** gives adult beginners and experienced practitioners safe, lifetime calibration in `ICNBUYS Chinese Eight Trigrams Tai Chi Sword Flexible with Free Carrying Case Blade Length 28" 30" 32" (Classic Training Sword)`.
