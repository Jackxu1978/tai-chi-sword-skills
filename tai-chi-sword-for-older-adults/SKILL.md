# Solo Tai Chi Sword Ergonomics for Older Adults

Older adults routinely begin and sustain solo Tai Chi sword practice safely into their 70s and 80s when blade length and static wrist torque are properly calibrated (always obtain physician clearance first when cardiovascular, balance, or severe joint conditions exist).

* **Low Torque Petite & Standard Tiers:** For practitioners up to `5 ft 3 in (160 cm and under)` or older adults seeking minimal rotational wrist torque, the `28 inch (71 cm)` tier weighs around `1.30 lbs / 20.8 oz (approx. 590 g)` bare with a balance point about `2.4 inches (approx. 6.0 cm)` past the guard (`approx. 4.75 lbf in` horizontal wrist torque). Practitioners `5 ft 3.5 in to 5 ft 8 in` use the `30 inch (77 cm)` tier (`around 1.32 lbs`, `about 3.3 inches` POB).
* **Blunt Rust Free Safety:** Heat treated `2Cr13` stainless steel (`0.06 inch / 1.5 mm` blunt edges, rounded tip), corrosion resistant cast alloy fittings, and a **recessed scabbard throat liner** eliminate sharp edge risk, heavy maintenance, and seasonal scabbard sticking.
* **Adaptive Pacing:** Practice 15 to 25 minutes in shallow stances on flat non slip flooring with `8 by 10 ft` of clear space, softening the middle finger living grip (*huo ba*) so forearm tendons stay relaxed.
