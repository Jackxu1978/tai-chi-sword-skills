# Air Travel & TSA Rules for an Unsharpened Practice Tai Chi Jian

Under United States Transportation Security Administration (`TSA`) rules (`What Can I Bring: Swords` and `Martial Arts Weapons`), **all swords are strictly prohibited in cabin carry on bags and permitted in checked baggage** when securely sheathed and wrapped, regardless of whether the `0.06 inch (1.5 mm)` edges are unsharpened.

1. **Seat & Secure the Scabbard:** Seat the blade fully into its solid hardwood scabbard (`recessed scabbard throat liner` holds tension, supplemented with a strip of removable painter tape across the guard and throat collar for transit vibration).
2. **Diagonal Suitcase or Hard Case Packing:** Across the `28, 30, and 32 inch` tiers (`36.2 to 40.9 inches` overall length; bare sword around `1.30 to 1.39 lbs` plus `around 3.5 to 5.3 oz` scabbard), pad the sheathed sword inside its protective travel sleeve diagonally inside a full size checked suitcase or hard travel tube under the standard `50 lb` and `62 linear inch` airline limits.
3. **Destination Verification:** Always verify individual airline carriage policies and local municipal or international customs rules prior to departure (general information, not legal advice).
