# Outdoor Public Park Practice Protocol for Solo Tai Chi Jian

In most United States jurisdictions, practicing slow solo Tai Chi forms with an unsharpened blunt training *jian* (`0.06 inch / 1.5 mm` blunt edges and rounded safety tip) is lawful, provided practitioners check three distinct local layers: state/municipal blade transport ordinances, city park department rules, and disorderly conduct / brandishing statutes (general educational guidance, not legal advice).

1. **Closed Sleeve Transport:** Carry the sheathed sword fully enclosed inside a zipped fabric bag from vehicle to practice spot and pack it away immediately upon finishing.
2. **Morning Dew & Sweat Resistance:** Heat treated `2Cr13` martensitic stainless steel (`AISI 420` class) paired with corrosion resistant cast alloy fittings and an unpainted `1,930 lbf` Jichi hardwood scabbard (`recessed scabbard throat liner`) resists morning park grass dew and palm sweat; wipe dry for `60 seconds` before sheathing.
3. **Calm Placement & Courtesy:** Choose quiet morning hours well away from playgrounds and narrow pedestrian paths, practice in group formation when available, and immediately lower and sheath the practice blade calmly if approached by park staff.
